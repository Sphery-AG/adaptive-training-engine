# Circuit templates: what the evidence says

Aug 5. Research pass over the 8 per-goal circuit templates, done before showing
them to Stephan. Each section: what the template does, what the literature
supports, and what we changed because of it. These stay drafts until Stephan
signs off; this doc is so that conversation starts from evidence, not vibes.

## The format itself holds up

Circuit training as a format (station rotation, short rests, moderate to high
intensity) has solid meta-analytic support: significant reductions in body
weight and fat mass, plus improvements in strength and VO2max, with the best
results at 3+ sessions per week. That matches our default of 3 sessions per
week and continuous station rotation.

- Seo et al. 2019, Obesity Reviews: weight loss effects of circuit training,
  systematic review + meta-analysis (avg -3.81 kg body weight).
- Ramos-Campo et al. 2021, Biology: resistance circuit-based training improves
  body composition, strength, and cardiorespiratory fitness (~4.3% fat mass
  reduction, VO2max +6.2%).

## Per goal

**Burn Circuit (Lose Weight & Burn Fat)** — intensity + strength + endurance
rotation. Directly matches the meta-analytic sweet spot above: moderate-to-high
intensity, short rests, mixed cardio/resistance. No changes needed.

**Strength Circuit (Build Strength & Muscle)** — three strength legs per
session. Resistance circuit training reliably improves strength and lean mass
(Ramos-Campo 2021; Nature Sci Reports 2024 for older adults). Honest caveat
for Stephan: pure hypertrophy programs favor longer rests than a circuit
allows; this is a strength circuit, not a bodybuilding block, and that is the
right product for a circle-training gym.

**Engine Circuit (Improve Fitness & Endurance)** — steady zone-3 volume with
one threshold leg. Interval + steady mix is the standard endurance prescription
(Gatorade Sports Science Institute / Sports Medicine HIIT reviews). Evidence
prefers fewer, longer quality sessions over many short ones, which fits 3
sessions per week.

**Foundation Circuit (Move Pain-Free)** — mobility-led, everything eased.
Cochrane (2021): exercise is probably effective for chronic low back pain vs
usual care (moderate certainty). Notable nuance: high-intensity training
outperformed moderate intensity in chronic nonspecific low back pain trials,
so permanent zone 1 is likely too cautious as the member progresses. Kept
conservative for unsupervised v1; flagged as a progression question for
Stephan. Changed: circuit now prefers the medical/rehab corner (ICAROS
Guardian, Medical Leg Press, Cable Pulls, Tidal Tanks, Bike).

**Longevity Circuit (Boost Health & Longevity)** — one leg each of cardio,
strength, balance, cognitive, recovery. Strongest evidence of all eight: WHO
and the ICFSR global consensus (2025) recommend exactly this multicomponent
mix for healthy aging (aerobic + resistance 2x/week + balance work 3x/week;
balance training cuts fall risk up to 40%; adding cognitive tasks improves
frailty markers). No changes needed.

**Performance Circuit (Improve Sports Performance)** — explosive legs bookend
reaction + strength work. Plyometric training meta-analyses show improvements
in sprint, jump, strength, and reactive strength index; combined
plyometric + balance/reactive programs beat either alone, and response-based
tasks (which is what XR Fighter is) are the most performance-relevant agility
stimulus. No changes needed.

**Race Prep Circuit (Prepare for an Event)** — REWORKED after this research.
HYROX's own training methodology is "compromised running": performing station
work immediately after running, again and again, because that is the race
(8 x 1 km run + station). Changes made: (1) the template now alternates
cardio and station legs so functional work always happens under aerobic
fatigue, and (2) the circuit prefers the real race stations (Runner, ergs,
Sled Push/Pull, Wall Balls, Sandbag Lunges, Farmers Carry, Burpee Broad Jump)
over the digital stations. On the Darmstadt floor a signature session now
resolves to: Row Erg warmup, then Runner, Sled Push, Ski Erg, Burpee Broad
Jump, Sled Pull, Row Erg cooldown. Relevant beyond members: this is the
circuit the Gold's Gym / MAG HYROX prospect sees on Aug 26.

**Dual Flow Circuit (Train Body & Mind)** — dual-task legs carry the session.
Sphery's home turf: the ExerCube HIIT study (Martin-Niedecken et al. 2020,
Frontiers in Computer Science) found exergame-based functional HIIT comparable
to conventional training while scoring higher on flow, enjoyment, motivation,
and cognitive load. Reviews of cognitive-motor dual-task training in healthy
adults show positive effects on cognitive and dual-task function. No changes
needed.

## Cross-cutting change from this pass

Balance-first equipment (ICAROS Guardian) can't drive real heart rate, so any
leg landing on it is now capped at zone 2 regardless of the session target.
